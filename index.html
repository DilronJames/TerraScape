A SMALL NOTE ON PRIVACY

Your garden stays yours.

Fieldnote analyzes your garden photo in this browser. It is not uploaded or saved. We do not request device location or send your typed location to a server; ZIP matching happens on this device.

Plant reference photos are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. That image request shares standard connection information with Wikimedia, but does not include your garden photo or location.

Photo clues and native plant suggestions are a starting point, not a site survey. Check your sunlight, soil moisture, drainage, mature plant spacing, and local planting guidance before buying.
