A SMALL NOTE

Good advice starts with context.

Your photo, location, and garden plan are handled in this browser. Photos are not uploaded or saved by Fieldnote. Fieldnote does not request device location or send your data to a server; anything you type is used only for on-device regional matching.

The photo scan estimates image brightness and green color. It’s only a rough cue, since photo exposure can’t tell us how much sun a planting spot gets. Recommendations are a starting point; check your spot and local planting guidance before planting. Saved concepts are created on this device when you choose to download them.
